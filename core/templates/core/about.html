Banking that grows with you

LuminaBank provides students with a modern, transparent, and low-cost banking experience that starts on campus and continues for life.

Our Mission

We believe every student deserves access to banking tools that are affordable, easy to use, and free from unnecessary fees. By partnering with colleges and universities, we deliver fast financial aid disbursements and a full-featured digital bank that students can keep long after graduation.

What makes us different

  • Student-first design — Built around the real financial moments of campus life: aid refunds, part-time jobs, textbooks, and the first full-time paycheck.
  • Transparent pricing — No overdraft fees. Clear fee schedule. Simple ways to manage costs.
  • Security by default — Card controls, zero liability protection, multi-factor authentication, and real-time alerts.
  • Financial empowerment — Tools and resources that help students build lasting money habits.
